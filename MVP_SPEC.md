# MVP Design Specification

## Thesis embodied by the game

The tower is not a morality meter. It is a physical record of whether consequential discrepancies can still reach the parts of a system capable of changing.

The player is always trading among:

- immediate task performance,
- structural stability,
- the accessibility of correction routes,
- the cost of review,
- and unresolved correction debt.

A maximally responsive tower can become unstable or nonfunctional. A maximally efficient tower can become sealed. The target is maintained, discriminating correction inside a system that can still act.

## Core loop

1. A discrepancy appears.
2. The player receives incomplete evidence, stakes, and recurrence context.
3. The player chooses a response.
4. The choice updates the system variables.
5. A new floor is procedurally constructed from the resulting state.
6. Previous choices constrain the next discrepancy.
7. A final crisis tests the architecture rather than the player’s stated intentions.

## Variables

| Symbol | Variable | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| P | Immediate performance | Current visible task output |
| R | Route accessibility | Reachability of implicated components from a discrepancy |
| S | Structural stability | Ability to preserve coherent operation under change |
| Debt | Correction debt | Unresolved dependencies carried forward by local fixes |
| φ | Fast adaptability | Capacity for local belief and policy adjustment |
| θ | Slow-coordinate rigidity | Resistance of values, priors, governance, and model class |
| M | Audit memory | Preservation of anomalies, dissent, rejected alternatives, and reasons |
| D | Dissent access | Ability of minority signals to retain force through escalation |
| I | Channel independence | Degree to which evidence channels add distinct information |
| C | Review capacity | Finite resources available for validation, audit, and escalation |

## Visual grammar

- Thick luminous central core: high route accessibility.
- Thin or dark core: correction pathway is narrowing.
- Opaque walls: high slow-coordinate rigidity.
- Open balconies: dissent retains access.
- Multiple antennas: partially independent feedback channels.
- Archive rings: retained anomaly and audit memory.
- Dark wedges: accumulated correction debt.
- Cages: containment that preserves the discrepancy.
- Bright structural halos: slow-coordinate or model-class revision.
- Twisted floors and orbiting arcs: overreaction and instability.
- Lateral drift: accumulated instability and path dependence.

## Endings

### The Adaptive Tower
The route remains usable, correction debt is controlled, the system can still perform, and change is proportionate.

### The Sealed Tower
Signals are processed but cannot alter governing assumptions.

### The Oscillating Tower
The system changes on every anomaly and cannot preserve enough stability to audit its own updates.

### The Fragile Hybrid
The system survives through narrow, conditional pathways that may fail in the next crisis.

### The Open Ruin
Nearly everything remains revisable, but the system has consumed its practical function through unbounded review and structural intervention.

## MVP completion criteria

- Ten playable narrative floors.
- Three meaningful choices per floor.
- Visible variable updates.
- Procedural 3D architecture reflecting system state.
- Clickable historical floors.
- X-ray slow-coordinate view.
- Multiple structurally computed endings.
- Desktop and mobile layout.
- No remote assets required in the standalone build.

## Expansion roadmap

### Phase 2 — Replay and comparison
- Seeded runs.
- Shareable tower codes.
- Side-by-side tower comparison.
- Full decision replay timeline.
- Explanations of counterfactual choices after completion.

### Phase 3 — Scenario authoring
- Visual scenario editor.
- Import/export scenario packs.
- Custom variables and thresholds.
- Empirical case packs with sources and evidence layers.

### Phase 4 — Multi-agent institution mode
- Separate engineering, management, audit, and external-oversight roles.
- Asymmetric evidence and incentives.
- Negotiated escalation and handoff.
- Group-level correction routes that no single player can see completely.

### Phase 5 — Research mode
- Export turn-level data.
- Pre-registered experimental conditions.
- Route-component ablations.
- Miss, overreaction, recurrence, cost, and response-delay measurements.

## Mobile and PWA completion criteria

- Safe-area-aware portrait and landscape layouts.
- A consequential response is visible without initial scrolling on phone portrait and landscape.
- Touch sheet gestures, horizontal choice rails, and minimum 44 CSS pixel action targets.
- Adaptive 3D quality with reduced battery cost on lower-power devices.
- Autosave, resume, undo, background pause, haptics, and optional wake lock.
- Installable manifest, maskable icon, Apple touch icon, and offline app-shell caching.
- Story-mode recovery when WebGL2 is unavailable.
